# Competitor Comparison

HyperBoostX is positioned as a safe AI Windows Gaming Optimizer, not as a replacement for official vendor apps or specialist overclocking/benchmark tools.

| Product | What They Do Better | HyperBoostX Advantage | Claim Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Razer Cortex | Mature game launcher and commercial polish | Local-first Safety Guard, restore visibility, AI explanations, report flow | Do not claim guaranteed FPS boost |
| Hone | Broad optimizer presets and tuning community | Safer plan-first approvals and explicit blocked dangerous actions | Do not copy unsafe tweak claims |
| Process Lasso | Deep process automation and ProBalance | Beginner-friendly process pressure explanations and protected-process blocking | Do not claim full Process Lasso parity |
| WinUtil | Broad Windows utility/debloat scope | Gaming-focused safe optimizer with restore/report workflow | Do not become destructive debloater |
| NVIDIA App | Official NVIDIA driver/features/overlay | Vendor-aware guide and conflict detection | Do not claim driver-level NVIDIA control |
| AMD Adrenalin | Official AMD driver tuning and HYPR-RX | Safe guidance and report-centered workflow | Do not replace Adrenalin |
| Intel Graphics Software | Official Intel Arc/iGPU controls | Unknown/iGPU fallback and beginner explanations | Do not claim official Intel feature control |
| MSI Afterburner | OC/undervolt/fan/OSD specialist | Safer non-OC optimization and restore-first UX | Do not expose OC/voltage tools |
| PresentMon | Low-level frame telemetry | Roadmap import/analysis surface | Do not claim built-in PresentMon capture yet |
| CapFrameX | Advanced capture/statistics and comparisons | Local report integration roadmap | Do not claim community benchmark database yet |
| OCAT | Lightweight frame capture | Benchmark import/export roadmap | Do not claim capture parity yet |

## Winning Position

HyperBoostX wins only where it is safer, clearer, reversible, beginner-friendly, and integrated across scan, plan, report, restore, and education. Specialist tools remain better at their specialist jobs.
